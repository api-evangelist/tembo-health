# Tembo Health

> Tembo Health is a telemedicine practice (founded 2018) that partners with skilled nursing facilities and senior living communities to deliver 24/7 virtual specialty and urgent care to elderly residents — including treat-in-place emergency medicine, psychiatry, cardiology, and dementia care. Tembo Health is a healthcare services provider and does not currently publish a public developer API, SDK, or documentation surface.
